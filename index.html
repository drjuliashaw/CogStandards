About

The AI Epistemic Impacts Initiative (EII) is a group of researchers working on the epistemic risks and virtues of AI. The aim is to understand the best ways to protect human minds from harm, and how AI can be used to strengthen humanity’s ability to make good decisions.

What we do

EII members work together to discuss new findings, share work in progress and publish research. The work covers both the ways AI can harm human thinking and the ways it can support better reasoning and decision-making.

Cognitive capacities worth protecting

EII uses the mental functions defined in the WHO International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF) as a map of which aspects of human cognition AI could harm or support.

Cognitive Security Task Force

A group based at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI that studies how emerging technologies affect human attention, perception, memory and judgement, and develops standards, safeguards and norms for cognitive security in the age of AI and brain-computer interfaces.
