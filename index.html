The Centre for Cognitive Guidelines in AI

Helping protect human cognition and agency from the potentially harmful effects of AI.

Why we need cognitive guidelines

To adequately prepare for a world with advanced AI, we must measure and protect core functions of the human mind.

Already we can see that AI systems are used as digital advisers, tutors, confidants, and therapists. These interactions have the potential to change how people think, what they believe, and how they make important decisions. This presents new and emerging risks.

CogGuide develops best practice guidelines to help frontier labs make responsible choices related to cognitive security, and to give policymakers and regulators a clear benchmark to hold them to.
