Research projects on cognitive priorities

Research

In addition to producing guidelines, CogGuide identifies open questions about how AI affects the mind and then runs original research to answer them.

Why we do original research

For many of the ways AI could affect mental functions there is insufficient evidence regarding precisely what the impacts are likely to be. Every review finds many ways in which the existing research could be supplemented, and works with experts to try and address those gaps.

How the research is done

This work follows standard academic practice. Each project is expected to be published in peer-reviewed academic journals and presented at academic conferences.
