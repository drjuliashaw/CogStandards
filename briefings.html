Cognitive Guideline Briefings

Briefings

Each cognitive guideline comes with short briefings for the people who act on it.

Why we write briefings

Cognitive guidelines are detailed documents written for expert panels and evaluators. The people who act on them often need something shorter. Developers need to know what to change in a model and how to test it. Policymakers and regulators need to understand the risk and how a guideline can be used as a benchmark. CogGuide briefings summarise each guideline for one of these audiences, so the evidence reaches the people who make decisions about AI.
